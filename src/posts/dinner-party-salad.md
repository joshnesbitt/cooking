This is the salad I make when people are coming round. It's incredibly basic and that's the point: three good ingredients and a proper [house dressing](/house-dressing), assembled with a bit of care. It goes with roast chicken, with [porchetta](/porchetta), with a barbecue, with nothing at all. The work is in the shopping, so buy ripe tomatoes that actually smell of tomato.

## Ingredients

* 500g heritage tomatoes, a mix of sizes and colours
* 2 heads of butter lettuce
* 2 ripe avocados
* 1 batch of [house dressing](/house-dressing)
* Maldon sea salt and black pepper to finish

## Method

1. Make the [house dressing](/house-dressing) recipe
1. Cut the tomatoes into irregular chunks and wedges, season with a pinch of Maldon and leave them for 10 minutes so the salt draws out their juices
1. Separate the lettuce into leaves, tearing the larger ones, then wash and dry them thoroughly
1. Halve, stone and thickly slice the avocados last so they don't brown
1. Toss the leaves gently with half the dressing using your hands
1. Layer the leaves, tomatoes (and their juices) and avocado on a large platter, spooning the remaining dressing over as you go
1. Finish with a final pinch of Maldon and a grind of pepper, and serve immediately

## Notes

Dry the lettuce properly. Dressing slides straight off wet leaves and pools in the bottom of the bowl, which is how salads end up bland and soggy at the same time. A salad spinner and then a clean tea towel is the way.

Salting the tomatoes ahead of time is doing two jobs: it seasons them through and the juices they release become part of the dressing on the platter.

Dress it at the last possible moment. Butter lettuce is delicate and starts wilting the second the acid hits it, so assemble the platter when everyone is already sitting down.
